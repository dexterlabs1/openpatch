libsamplerate 0.1.9 — mono SRC_SINC_FASTEST adaptation

Upstream: https://github.com/libsndfile/libsamplerate
Release tag: 0.1.9
Immutable commit: f6730d03c3e7660bb6ecad8816f1b09c5825142a

OpenPatch adapts the mono streaming sinc algorithm, fixed-point interpolation,
ratio transitions, buffer management and reset behavior into pure Rust, and
includes the fastest coefficient table as exact IEEE-754 binary32 values.
