Cross Matter / Warps source adaptation
======================================

Covered OpenPatch files: openpatch-core/src/cross_matter/kernel.rs and
openpatch-core/src/cross_matter/kernel/*.rs.

Adapted into Rust from Warps and stmlib by Emilie Gillet:
eurorack f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
stmlib e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86/warps
https://github.com/pichenettes/stmlib/tree/e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec

Modified Rust source and generated bit-preserving lookup, filter-bank and
converter tables, with an original OpenPatch native adapter, 96 kHz rate bridge
and panel. No Mutable Instruments artwork, logo, analog hardware, codec/CV
calibration or firmware UI is incorporated. Source inventory and file hashes:
scripts/warps_reference/source_manifest.json. The voltage scaling, CV mapping
and host-rate resampling are explicit OpenPatch adaptations; this does not
claim complete firmware or hardware identity.
