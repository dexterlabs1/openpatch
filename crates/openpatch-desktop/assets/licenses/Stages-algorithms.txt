Segment Garden / Stages source adaptation
========================================

Covered files: openpatch-core/src/segment_garden/kernel.rs and kernel_resources.rs.
Portable STANDARD3 segment generator, Stages resource tables, Tides ramp extractor,
and stmlib DSP/quantizer/delay/RNG helpers.
eurorack: f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
stmlib: e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86/stages

OpenPatch supplies original native fixed-clock bridging, physical cable grouping,
ordered edge capture, shared module RNG/physical note-state ownership, ideal voltage
scaling, saved controls and shared rack/browser UI. Endpoint and capacity guards
are declared native extensions. ALT audio firmware, chain expanders, hardware ADC,
DAC calibration, panel artwork and logos are outside this adaptation. Source-defined
qualification and native timing limits are separate; see docs/SEGMENT_GARDEN.md and
scripts/stages_reference/source_manifest.json.
