Frame Scene / Frames source adaptation
=====================================

Covered OpenPatch files: openpatch-core/src/frame_scene/kernel.rs and tables.rs.
Adapted from Frames keyframer.{h,cc}, resources.{h,cc} and stmlib/stmlib.h.
eurorack: f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
stmlib: e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86/frames

The fixed native_gain.rs table is a declared ideal inverse of the source
response-zero DAC compensation curve. It is not measured analog calibration.
OpenPatch supplies original structured authoring, host timing, mixer routing,
validation and UI. Native array guards and wrapping extend source-undefined
boundary domains; only sanitizer-audited defined cases establish equivalence.
No Mutable Instruments panel artwork, logo, firmware storage or analog circuit
calibration is included. Exact source files/hashes and full qualification scope:
scripts/frames_reference/source_manifest.json and docs/FRAME_SCENE.md.
