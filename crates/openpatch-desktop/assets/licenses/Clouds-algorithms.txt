Grain Weather / Clouds source adaptation
========================================

Covered OpenPatch files: openpatch-core/src/grain_weather/kernel.rs and
openpatch-core/src/grain_weather/kernel/*.rs.

Adapted into Rust from Clouds (original firmware; no Parasites or other
alternate firmware) and stmlib by Emilie Gillet:
eurorack f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
stmlib e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86/clouds
https://github.com/pichenettes/stmlib/tree/e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
Window::Start restores the done_ assignment present in clouds/dsp/window.h
up to eurorack 2a468734bdd53bea0f25165bf34d92a4c571db13 (same author, same
license).

Modified Rust source (granular processor, recording buffers and mu-law codec,
granular, WSOLA and looping players, correlator, diffuser, reverb, pitch
shifter, sample-rate converters, STFT phase vocoder with the ShyFFT and the
frame transformation) and generated bit-preserving lookup and filter tables,
with an original OpenPatch native adapter, 32 kHz rate bridge and panel. No
Mutable Instruments artwork, logo, analog hardware, codec/CV calibration,
sample storage or firmware UI is incorporated. Source inventory and file
hashes: scripts/clouds_reference/source_manifest.json. The voltage scaling, CV
mapping, main-loop cadence and host-rate resampling are explicit OpenPatch
adaptations; this does not claim complete firmware or hardware identity.
