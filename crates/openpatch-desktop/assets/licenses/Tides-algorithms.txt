Tidal Functions / Tides 2018 source adaptation
=============================================

Covered OpenPatch files: openpatch-core/src/tidal_functions/kernel.rs,
openpatch-core/src/tidal_functions/kernel/*.rs, and
openpatch-core/src/tidal_functions/extractor.rs.

Adapted into Rust from Tides 2018 and stmlib by Emilie Gillet:
eurorack f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
stmlib e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86/tides2
https://github.com/pichenettes/stmlib/tree/e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec

Modified Rust source and generated bit-preserving lookup tables, with an
original OpenPatch native adapter and panel. No Mutable Instruments artwork,
logo, analog hardware, ADC/DAC calibration or firmware UI is incorporated.
Source inventory and file hashes: scripts/tides2_reference/source_manifest.json.
The native block timing, control mapping and startup safety policy are explicit
OpenPatch adaptations; this does not claim complete firmware or hardware identity.
